# Title: Right to Information Application Procedure under Section 6 of RTI Act 2005
# Source URL: https://rtionline.gov.in/
# Date Accessed: 19 Nov 2026
# Scope: Central & State Public Authorities

## 1. Application Submission
Under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, any citizen of India may request information from a Public Authority.
- The request must be made in writing or through electronic means in English, Hindi, or in the official language of the area in which the application is being made.
- The applicant must specify the particulars of the information sought.
- The application must be accompanied by the prescribed application fee (₹10 for Central public authorities).

## 2. Addressee of Application
- The application should be addressed to the Central Public Information Officer (CPIO) or State Public Information Officer (SPIO) of the concerned department.
- If submitted to an Assistant Public Information Officer (APIO), an additional 5 days is added to the statutory response timeline.

## 3. Assistance to Citizens
Under Section 6(2), an applicant making request for information shall not be required to give any reason for requesting the information or any other personal details except those that may be necessary for contacting them.
Under Section 6(3), where an application is made to a public authority requesting information which is held by another public authority, the public authority shall transfer the application to that other public authority within 5 days from receipt and immediately inform the applicant.

## 4. Statutory Timelines
Under Section 7(1):
- General requests: Information must be provided or rejected within 30 days from the date of receipt.
- Life and Liberty: Where the information sought concerns the life or liberty of a person, it must be provided within 48 hours of receipt.
- Failure to give decision within 30 days is deemed a refusal under Section 7(2).
