# Title: RTI First Appeal Procedure under Section 19 of RTI Act 2005
# Source URL: https://cic.gov.in/
# Date Accessed: 19 Nov 2026
# Scope: Central & State Public Authorities

## 1. Grounds for First Appeal
Under Section 19(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, any person who:
- Does not receive a decision within the specified 30 days time limit under Section 7(1), or
- Is aggrieved by a decision of the Central Public Information Officer (CPIO) or State Public Information Officer (SPIO) regarding rejection, incomplete information, or excessive fee demand,
may prefer a First Appeal.

## 2. Limitation Period (Deadline)
- The appeal must be filed within 30 days from the expiry of the 30-day response period or within 30 days from the receipt of the PIO's decision.
- The First Appellate Authority (FAA) may admit the appeal after the expiry of 30 days if satisfied that the appellant was prevented by sufficient cause.

## 3. Appellate Authority
- The appeal is made to an officer senior in rank to the CPIO/SPIO in the same public authority (designated as the First Appellate Authority - FAA).
- There is no application fee for filing a First Appeal before Central Government authorities.

## 4. Timelines for Disposal
Under Section 19(6):
- The appeal shall be disposed of within 30 days of receipt.
- In exceptional cases, for reasons to be recorded in writing, the disposal period may be extended up to a maximum of 45 days from the date of filing.
- If aggrieved by the FAA order, a Second Appeal lies to the Central Information Commission (CIC) or State Information Commission (SIC) within 90 days under Section 19(3).
