# Title: Traffic e-Challan Citizen Dispute and Online Grievance Procedure
# Source URL: https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in/
# Date Accessed: 19 Nov 2026
# Scope: All States & Union Territories (Ministry of Road Transport and Highways)

## 1. Viewing and Verification of e-Challan
Citizens can check traffic violations and pending citations on the central MoRTH e-Challan portal (echallan.parivahan.gov.in) using:
- Challan Number, or
- Vehicle Registration Number (with last 5 digits of Chassis/Engine number), or
- Driving License Number.

## 2. Challenging an Incorrect or Wrongly Issued Challan
If an e-Challan is issued erroneously (e.g. cloned number plate, wrong vehicle photograph, camera calibration error, or emergency vehicle exemption):
1. Navigate to the "Complaint" / "Grievance" section on the e-Challan portal.
2. Enter the Challan Number, Vehicle Number, and citizen contact details.
3. Select the grievance category (e.g. "Wrong Vehicle Tagged", "Duplicate Payment", "Incorrect Violation Location").
4. Upload documentary evidence: photograph of the actual vehicle, Registration Certificate (RC), or GPS/toll logs proving the vehicle was elsewhere at the time of the violation.
5. The grievance is routed to the Traffic Police Enforcement Branch of the concerned district/commissionerate for verification.

## 3. Disputed Challans and Virtual Court Referral
- If the citizen contests the violation and refuses online compounding, the challan is marked as "Contested" and transferred to the Virtual Court / Judicial Magistrate having jurisdiction under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
- Citizens are issued a summon notice with a hearing date or can submit their defence online before the Virtual Court Magistrate.
- Paying the fine constitutes compounding the offense under Section 200 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, after which no further dispute can be entertained.
