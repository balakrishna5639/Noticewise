# Title: TSSPDCL Consumer Complaint and Billing Dispute Resolution Procedure
# Source URL: https://tgsouthernpower.org/
# Date Accessed: 19 Nov 2026
# State: Telangana (Southern Power Distribution Company of Telangana Limited)

## 1. Disputed Bill Submission
When a consumer receives an inflated electricity bill, unexpected arrears demand, or disputed tariff classification from TSSPDCL:
- The consumer must file a formal complaint in writing or through the TSSPDCL mobile app / web portal before the Assistant Engineer (AE - Operations) or Section Officer having jurisdiction.
- Quote the unique Consumer Service Connection Number (USC Number), consumer name, billing month, and clear grounds of dispute (e.g. meter reading error, stuck meter, wrong category).

## 2. Provisional Payment Requirement During Dispute
- Under TSERC Consumer Service Regulations, filing a complaint does not grant automatic stay of disconnection.
- To prevent disconnection during the investigation, the consumer is generally required to pay an amount equal to the average of the previous three undisputed months' consumption, pending final dispute resolution.

## 3. Rectification and Refund Procedure
- TSSPDCL staff must inspect the meter and verify billing records within 7 days of complaint registration.
- If billing error or meter defect is established, revised bill with credit adjustment is issued in the immediate subsequent billing cycle.
- If the Section Officer fails to resolve the dispute within 15 days, the consumer may escalate the complaint to the Divisional Engineer (DE) or the Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF).
