# Title: TSERC Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF) and Ombudsman Regulations
# Source URL: https://cgrf.tgsouthernpower.org/
# Date Accessed: 19 Nov 2026
# State: Telangana (Telangana State Electricity Regulatory Commission - Regulation No. 3 of 2015)

## 1. Establishment and Jurisdiction of CGRF
Every distribution licensee in Telangana (TSSPDCL and TSNPDCL) maintains a Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF) comprising a Chairperson and Technical/Independent members.
- CGRF has jurisdiction over complaints relating to billing disputes, delay in power supply restoration, meter inaccuracies, and deficiency of service.
- The Forum does NOT entertain matters pending before courts, assessment of unauthorized use under Section 126, or theft under Section 135 of Electricity Act 2003.

## 2. Procedure for Filing Grievance before CGRF
1. A consumer aggrieved by non-resolution by DISCOM internal officers may file a petition before CGRF in the prescribed Form.
2. Provide copy of original complaint, DISCOM reply (if any), consumer service connection number, and supporting documents.
3. No fee is payable for filing a grievance before CGRF.
4. CGRF provides acknowledgment within 3 days and conducts hearings.

## 3. Disposal Timeline
- CGRF must pass a reasoned final order within 45 days from the date of receipt of the grievance.
- The licensee (DISCOM) must implement the CGRF order within the timeframe specified in the order (typically 15 to 30 days).

## 4. Representation to Electricity Ombudsman
- Any consumer aggrieved by non-redressal of grievance by CGRF or non-implementation of CGRF order by DISCOM may submit a representation to the Electricity Ombudsman.
- Limitation: The appeal to the Ombudsman must be preferred within 30 days from the date of receipt of CGRF order.
