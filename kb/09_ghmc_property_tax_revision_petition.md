# Title: GHMC Property Tax Assessment Objection and Revision Petition under Section 282
# Source URL: https://www.ghmc.gov.in/
# Date Accessed: 19 Nov 2026
# State: Telangana (Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955)

## 1. Statutory Right to File Revision Petition
Under Section 282 of the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) Act, 1955:
- Any property owner or taxpayer aggrieved by a new property tax assessment, enhanced annual rental value (ARV), or a Special Notice of property tax demand has the statutory right to file a Revision Petition (Objection Petition).

## 2. Limitation Period (Deadline)
- The Revision Petition must be filed within 30 days from the date of service of the Special Notice or bill of demand.
- Petitions filed after 30 days are liable to be dismissed as time-barred unless condoned for sufficient recorded reasons.

## 3. Grounds for Objection
A revision petition may challenge the assessment on grounds including:
- Inaccurate plinth / built-up area measurement.
- Erroneous classification of property usage (e.g. assessing residential premises as commercial).
- Incorrect zone or sub-zone categorization for Monthly Rental Value (MRV).
- Disregard of statutory age depreciation for older buildings.
- Vacancy remission claim where premises remained unoccupied.

## 4. Documents to Submit
- Copy of GHMC Special Notice / Property Tax Assessment Notice.
- Registered Sale Deed or Title Deed copy proving ownership.
- Sanctioned building plan or architect plinth area measurement certificate.
- Electricity bill and property tax receipts for previous years.
- Photographs of property showing current actual usage.

## 5. Disposal and Hearing
- The Deputy Commissioner / Assessment Authority must issue a notice of hearing giving the taxpayer an opportunity to be heard in person or via authorized representative.
- A reasoned written order reducing, confirming, or revising the assessment must be passed.
