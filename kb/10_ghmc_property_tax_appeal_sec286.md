# Title: GHMC Property Tax Appeal Procedure to Civil Court under Section 286
# Source URL: https://cdma.cgg.gov.in/
# Date Accessed: 19 Nov 2026
# State: Telangana (Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955)

## 1. Appeal before the Chief Judge, City Civil Court
Under Section 286 of the GHMC Act, 1955:
- An appeal against any order passed by the Deputy Commissioner upon a revision petition under Section 282 lies to the Chief Judge, City Civil Court, Hyderabad.

## 2. Pre-conditions for Filing Appeal
- The appeal must be presented within 30 days from the date of the order disposing of the revision petition.
- Pre-requisite condition: The appellant must have first filed a revision petition under Section 282 and had it decided by the municipal authority. An appeal cannot be made directly without exhausting the Section 282 revision remedy.
- Statutory Pre-deposit: The taxpayer must deposit with the Commissioner the undisputed tax amount or the percentage prescribed under the Act pending the appeal.

## 3. High Court Remedy
- If either party is aggrieved by the judgment of the Chief Judge, City Civil Court under Section 286, an appeal or revision lies to the High Court for the State of Telangana on questions of law.
