# Title: National Food Security Act 2013 - Chapter VII Grievance Redressal and Appeal Procedure
# Source URL: https://nfsa.gov.in/
# Date Accessed: 19 Nov 2026
# Scope: Central & State Targeted Public Distribution System (Ration Cards)

## 1. Statutory Grievance Redressal Mechanism
Under Chapter VII of the National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013:
- Section 14 mandates an internal grievance redressal mechanism (toll-free helpline, citizen service portals) for each State Food and Civil Supplies Department.
- Section 15 mandates the appointment of a District Grievance Redressal Officer (DGRO) for each district to hear complaints regarding non-issuance, arbitrary cancellation, or wrongful rejection of ration cards and subsidized foodgrains.

## 2. Challenging Ration Card Rejection or Ineligibility Intimation
If an applicant receives an intimation rejecting an NFSA Food Security Card (Ration Card) or pension benefits:
1. File an application/appeal before the District Grievance Redressal Officer (usually the District Supply Officer or Joint Collector) within 30 days of receiving the rejection notice.
2. Specify the erroneous rejection grounds (e.g. wrongful presumption of landholding, faulty exclusion criteria, income certificate discrepancies, or Aadhaar mismatch).
3. Attach supporting evidence: family income certificate issued by Tahsildar, residential proof, Aadhaar cards of all family members, and electricity bill showing domestic connection within quota.

## 3. Hearing and Disposal by DGRO
- The DGRO conducts summary inquiry, summons records from the local Tahsildar / Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO), and passes written disposal orders within 30 days.
- If aggrieved by the DGRO order, an appeal lies to the State Food Commission under Section 16 within 30 days.
