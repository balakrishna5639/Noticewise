# Title: Telangana Aasara Social Security Pension Ineligibility and Appeal Guidelines
# Source URL: https://aasara.telangana.gov.in/
# Date Accessed: 19 Nov 2026
# State: Telangana (Panchayat Raj & Rural Development Department - G.O.Ms.No. 17)

## 1. Scope of Aasara Pension Scheme
The Telangana Government provides monthly social security pensions under the "Aasara" scheme to Old Age Pensioners (OAP), Widows, Disabled Persons (TOD), Weavers, and Toddy Tappers meeting designated socio-economic criteria.

## 2. Ineligibility Notices and Rejection Grounds
Applications for Aasara pension or existing beneficiaries may receive rejection/disqualification notices citing exclusion criteria:
- Ownership of large agricultural land (exceeding 3 acres wetland or 7.5 acres dryland).
- Presence of an earning government or formal private sector employee in the family.
- Ownership of four-wheeler vehicle (excluding one commercial taxi/auto).
- Annual family income exceeding rural/urban poverty limits as per civil supplies records.

## 3. Appeal Procedure for Rejected Applicants
If an eligible applicant is wrongly rejected or their pension is suspended:
1. File an appeal representation before the Project Director, District Rural Development Agency (DRDA) or District Collector.
2. In urban municipal areas, submit the appeal through the Municipal Commissioner / Ward Office.
3. Submit within 30 days from the date of the rejection notice.
4. Attach mandatory certificates:
   - Gram Sabha / Ward verification certificate of vulnerability.
   - Non-ownership certificate from Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO) for disputed land/vehicles.
   - Medical Board (SADAREM) disability certificate (for disability pension claims).
   - Age verification proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Birth certificate).
5. The District Collectorate conducts re-verification and issues an inclusion order if eligibility criteria are fulfilled.
